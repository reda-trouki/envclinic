# Code of Conduct

We want envclinic to be a welcoming project for everyone.

**Expected behavior**

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear.

**Unacceptable behavior**

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's security contact or a maintainer's profile.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
